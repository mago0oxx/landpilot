## 🌎 Overview

LandPilot is a professional platform designed to help real estate investors analyze land opportunities using data, financial analysis, geographic intelligence and artificial intelligence.

Instead of simply listing properties, LandPilot provides objective investment analysis to support better decisions.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Help investors identify high-potential land opportunities while reducing investment risk through technology and data-driven analysis.

---

## ✨ Vision

Become the most trusted platform for land investment analysis in the United States.
