# Purpose

The LPS Engine is the core decision-making system of LandPilot.

Its purpose is to evaluate residential land investment opportunities using objective data, financial analysis, market intelligence, environmental risk assessment, and development feasibility.

The engine does not predict the future.

Instead, it measures the quality of an investment opportunity based on the information available.

---

# Philosophy

Every investment decision should be supported by facts.

The engine must remain transparent, explainable, and continuously improvable.

Artificial Intelligence assists the investor but never replaces verified information.

---

# Engine Architecture

The LPS Engine is composed of multiple independent Intelligence Engines.

Each engine analyzes one dimension of the investment.

The final recommendation is generated only after every engine completes its analysis.

---

# Intelligence Engines

1. Location Intelligence

2. Development Intelligence

3. Financial Intelligence

4. Environmental Intelligence

5. Market Intelligence

6. Legal Intelligence

7. Infrastructure Intelligence

8. Decision Intelligence

---

# Output

Every analysis produces:

- LPS Score
- Confidence Level
- Risk Level
- Investment Recommendation
- Detailed Explanation

---

# Guiding Principle

The engine never asks:

"Is this land beautiful?"

It asks:

"Is this investment financially and strategically attractive?"
