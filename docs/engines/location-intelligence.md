# Purpose

The Location Intelligence Engine evaluates the attractiveness of a property's location for residential real estate investment.

It measures the quality of the surrounding area using objective indicators that influence long-term property value, residential demand, and future appreciation.

The engine does not evaluate financial profitability, zoning regulations, or environmental risks.

Its responsibility is exclusively to determine whether the location itself represents a strong investment opportunity.

---

# Business Question

Is this location attractive for residential real estate investment?

---

# Guiding Principles

- Every metric must be objective.
- Every score must be explainable.
- Data quality is more important than data quantity.
- No subjective opinions are allowed.
- Every factor should influence long-term investment performance.

---

# Engine Responsibilities

The Location Intelligence Engine is responsible for evaluating:

- Neighborhood quality
- Accessibility
- Population growth
- Employment growth
- School quality
- Crime level
- Nearby services
- Transportation
- Future development
- Residential desirability

It is NOT responsible for evaluating:

- Construction costs
- Flood risk
- Zoning
- Taxes
- ROI
- Utilities

Those dimensions belong to other Intelligence Engines.
